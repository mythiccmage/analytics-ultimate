# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Downloads are now split by how the click arrived: from a page on one of your sites (a real button click) or direct (shared links, scanners, bots). Shown next to the counts on the Overview and Downloads tabs.
- New "My IP addresses" setting: visits and downloads from the addresses or ranges you list are ignored, so your own devices stay out of your stats even without the exclude cookie. One click adds your current address. Addresses are only compared, never stored.
- A link back to the project in the footer of every page, and an optional "Show your support" snippet in Settings (nothing is added to your pages unless you paste it).
- Existing databases upgrade themselves on first load.

## 1.0.1
- Every tab now shows the numbers first, with the controls underneath: the project editor moved to the Projects tab, the tracked-link manager sits below the download counts, and the tracking line sits below the Overview.

## 1.0.0
- First release: pageviews, visitors, returning visitors, traffic sources, file downloads and custom events.
- Many sites, subdomains and domains in one install.
- Projects: group pages and downloads into products or sections, with month-by-month trends.
- Tracked download links that count on the server and redirect to the file.
- Web setup page, password login with throttling, exclude-my-browser, CSV export, data cleanup.
